Hirer Access & Facility Guide

Practical instructions for entering, operating heating & lighting, and securing the hall.

Key Code & Online Booking Check-In

Your unique 4-digit key safe PIN code will be sent via email 48 hours prior to your reservation date once full payment is verified.

1. Hall Access & Keysafe

Arrival & Entry

The external digital key safe is located to the right side of the main front porch door.

  • Pull down the black protective cover to reveal the dials.
  • Enter your 4-digit PIN, then press down the release latch to open.
  • Unlock both the upper mortice and lower Yale deadlocks.
  • Crucial: Keep the keys safe with you throughout the event, or return them immediately to the safe if people are freely entering.

2. Heating & Climate Controls

Warmth & Ventilation

The heating is thermostatically managed to ensure ambient comfort during all booked sessions.

  • The main digital thermostat is located on the wall next to the kitchen door.
  • To boost warmth, press the "1 Hr Boost" button once (indicated by a green LED).
  • Please do not attempt to unscrew radiator valves or tamper with the boiler cupboard in the utility room.
  • Ensure all high ventilation sash windows are securely closed before departing.

3. Sound System, Screen & Broadband

Audio, Visual & Connectivity

The hall is equipped with fast fibre broadband, our own PA system with wireless microphone, and a projector screen.

  • Broadband: Fast fibre Wi-Fi is available throughout the hall; network details are displayed in the entrance lobby.
  • PA & Microphone: Our PA amplifier and wireless microphone are stored in the locked AV cabinet (key is on the hall key ring).
  • Projector Screen: Fitted for presentations and film screenings (please lower and retract gently).
  • Please remember to switch off all auxiliary amplifiers and safely stow microphones before leaving.

4. Kitchen & Dishwasher Use

Food Preparation & Care

Our small kitchen is equipped with an oven and hobs, dishwasher, fridge, ample cutlery, and food prep surfaces (please note this is not a commercial kitchen).

  • Oven & Hobs: Ensure appliances are wiped clean and fully turned off after use.
  • Dishwasher: Turn on main power isolator; run cycles as needed, drain and power off after final load.
  • Cutlery & Prep Surfaces: Ample cutlery and prep surfaces are available; please wash, dry, and return items to drawers and cupboards.
  • Ensure the refrigerator is completely emptied of all Hirer food and beverage items.

End of Hire – Departure Checklist

Please complete these checks to ensure your full security deposit is refunded promptly.

Emergency Assistance During Hire: If you encounter an urgent building issue (e.g. water leak or lock failure), please contact the trustees via email at admin@saxbyvillagehall.co.uk or the contact details provided in your booking confirmation.
