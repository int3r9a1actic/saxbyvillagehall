A Warm, Welcoming Venue for Every Occasion

Nestled in the picturesque conservation village of Saxby All Saints, our historic village hall offers well-equipped kitchen amenities, full accessibility, and flexible hire for weddings, family parties, fitness classes, and community gatherings.
