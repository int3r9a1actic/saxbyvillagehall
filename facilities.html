Hall Facilities & Specifications

Detailed room dimensions, catering kitchen amenities, furniture inventory, and accessibility.

Primary Space

The Main Hall

A bright, airy hall featuring polished solid timber flooring, high vaulted ceilings, large timber-framed windows providing abundant natural daylight, and dimmable heritage chandelier lighting.

Dimensions 12.5m x 6.2m (approx. 78 m²)
Ceiling Height 3.8m to apex
Theatre / Standing 100 persons max
Seated Banqueting 80 persons seated

Ideal for dinner dances, yoga and pilates classes, musical performances, craft fairs, and wedding receptions.

Main Function Hall

Timber floor, acoustic wall treatment & dimmable chandeliers.

Fully Equipped

Modern Catering Kitchen

Directly adjoining the main hall with a wide roller shutter service hatch. Refurbished to commercial hygiene standards with seamless stainless steel work surfaces and non-slip safety flooring.

  • Commercial Fast Dishwasher: 3-minute rapid wash cycle with chemicals auto-dosed.
  • Cooking Appliances: Double electric fan oven, ceramic electric induction hob, and high-wattage microwave.
  • Refrigeration: Tall upright catering refrigerator plus secondary counter freezer.
  • Water Heating: Instant boiling water urn (5L) plus secondary tabletop burco boiler for large gatherings.
  • Crockery & Glassware: Complete matching inventory of 100 dinner plates, side plates, bowls, teacups, saucers, water tumblers, wine glasses, and cutlery.
Commercial Catering Kitchen

Stainless prep tables, rapid dishwasher, and serving hatch.

Full Accessibility & Amenities

We are committed to making Saxby All Saints Village Hall welcoming and accessible to all visitors:

  • Gentle ramped step-free access to main front entrance porch.
  • Wide double doorway with level thresholds throughout the building.
  • Dedicated accessible toilet with grab bars, emergency alarm pull cord, and fold-down baby changing unit.
  • Assistance dogs welcome throughout all areas.

Parking & Outdoor Grounds

Our rural setting includes convenient on-site and adjacent parking conveniences:

  • Dedicated hardstanding parking bays immediately alongside the hall.
  • Designated disabled blue badge parking space adjacent to the entrance ramp.
  • Ample unrestricted on-street parking along Church Lane (please park considerately).
  • Front lawn garden area suitable for welcome drinks during summer celebrations.
