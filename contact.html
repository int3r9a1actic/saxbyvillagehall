Location & Directions

Saxby All Saints is situated in North Lincolnshire, just off the B1204 midway between South Ferriby and Bonby, easily accessible from the A15 and M180.

Getting Here:
  • From Barton-upon-Humber (15 mins): Follow B1204 south through South Ferriby and Horkstow into Saxby All Saints.
  • From Brigg (15 mins): Travel north via the A18 and B1204 through Worlaby and Bonby.
  • Parking: On-site designated bays immediately adjacent to hall plus Blue Badge bay.
