Terms & Conditions of Hire

Standard hiring conditions governing the use, safety, and care of Saxby All Saints Village Hall.

Hirer Instructions
Agreement Notice: By submitting a booking form or confirming a reservation via Hall Booking Online, the named Hirer accepts these Standard Conditions of Hire on behalf of their party or organization.

1. Supervision & Hirer Responsibility

The Hirer must be at least 21 years of age and must be in personal attendance throughout the period of hire. The Hirer is responsible for:

  • The supervision of the premises, the fabric, and the contents against damage or neglect.
  • The behavior of all persons using the premises whatever their capacity, including proper supervision of car parking to avoid obstruction of the highway or neighboring properties.
  • Ensuring the maximum permitted hall capacity of 70 persons is strictly adhered to at all times.

2. Fire Safety & Emergency Procedures

The Hirer must familiarize themselves with the location and operation of fire exits, fire alarms, and fire extinguishers prior to commencement of their event:

  • All escape routes and emergency exits must be kept completely clear of tables, chairs, decorations, or other obstructions.
  • No naked flames (including candles, tea-lights, or indoor fireworks) are permitted inside the hall without prior written consent from the Management Committee.
  • The use of smoke/haze machines is strictly prohibited as they trigger the optical smoke detection systems.
  • In the event of a fire, the hall must be evacuated immediately to the designated assembly point across Church Lane, and the Fire Service called by dialing 999.

3. Licensing, Alcohol & Gaming

Saxby All Saints Village Hall holds a Premises Licence for regulated entertainment (music, dancing, and theatrical performances), but does not hold a permanent alcohol licence.

  • Bringing Own Alcohol: If alcohol is consumed on a complimentary or bring-your-own (BYO) basis with no charge, no additional licence is required.
  • Sale of Alcohol: If alcohol is to be sold or included in the price of admission, the Hirer must obtain a Temporary Event Notice (TEN) from North Lincolnshire Council and present a copy to the Booking Secretary at least 14 days prior to the event.
  • No illegal drugs, betting, or unapproved lottery gaming is permitted on the premises.

4. Noise & Neighbour Consideration

The hall is situated within a peaceful residential conservation village. Hirers must ensure minimal disturbance to local residents:

  • Doors and windows must remain closed during amplified music performances or DJ sets.
  • All music and entertainment must cease promptly by 11:30 PM on Fridays and Saturdays, and by 10:30 PM on weekdays and Sundays.
  • Guests must leave the premises quietly and vacate the premises and car parking spaces by midnight.

5. Care of Premises, Cleaning & Rubbish Removal

The hall is hired on a self-service basis. At the conclusion of the hire period:

  • Tables and chairs must be wiped down and neatly stacked in their designated storage areas as found.
  • Floors in the main hall, lobby, kitchen, and toilets must be swept (and mopped if any spills have occurred).
  • All kitchen surfaces, appliances, ovens, and worktops must be left completely clean and empty.
  • Rubbish & Recycling: The hall has limited waste collection. Hirers are strictly required to bag and take all event rubbish, food waste, bottles, and recycling home with them.

6. Deposits, Payments & Cancellation Policy

Hire charges must be paid in accordance with the following terms:

  • A deposit of £50.00 is payable to confirm private function bookings.
  • Full hire payment is due no later than 14 days before the scheduled date.
  • Cancellation by Hirer: Cancellations made more than 14 days prior receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 14 days may forfeit 50% of the hire fee unless the date is successfully re-booked.
  • Cancellation by Committee: The Committee reserves the right to cancel any hire agreement in the event of an emergency, severe weather, or use of the hall as an official polling station, in which case all fees paid will be refunded in full.

7. Insurance & Indemnity

The Village Hall holds Public Liability Insurance covering claims arising from defects in the hall building or fixtures:

  • The Committee accepts no liability for personal property, gifts, equipment, or vehicles brought onto or left on the premises.
  • Commercial hirers, class instructors, and third-party vendors (such as caterers or bouncy castle operators) must hold their own valid Public Liability Insurance (£2,000,000 minimum indemnity) and provide proof upon request.
  • The Hirer shall indemnify the Committee against any damage to the hall or claims by third parties arising from the Hirer's use of the premises.
