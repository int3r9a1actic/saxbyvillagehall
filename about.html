Our History & Heritage

Discover the story behind Saxby All Saints Village Hall – the beating heart of our historic conservation village.

Heritage Story

Rooted in North Lincolnshire Rural Life

Saxby All Saints is a picturesque estate village situated in the lowlands below the western ridge of the Lincolnshire Wolds. Celebrated for its red brick cottages, mature beech trees, and Grade II listed architecture, the village has preserved its peaceful rural character for generations.

The hall was originally constructed in 1846 as the village school, founded and funded through the generous patronage of the prominent local landed Hope-Barton family, who shaped much of the surrounding estate. For over a century, the schoolhouse served at the very heart of parish life, educating and nurturing generations of local children until its closure in 1961.

Following the school's closure, the historic Victorian building was gifted to the village community. Through the dedication of resident trustees, it was formally transitioned into a perpetual, community-owned village hall and registered charity (No. 521977)—safeguarding this cherished landmark as a welcoming gathering place for social events, celebrations, and community welfare.

Charitable Purpose

Our Role in the Community

Operating as a registered non-profit trust (Charity No. 521977) under Scheme of the Charity Commission.

Social Well-being & Health

Providing an accessible space for fitness classes, mental health social hubs, and weekly gatherings that combat rural isolation.

Parish Fellowship

Hosting seasonal celebrations, parish council assemblies, church teas, harvest suppers, and educational societies.

Affordable Private Hire

Offering subsidized hire rates to ensure local families have a top-tier venue for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, and christenings.

Village Schoolhouse Built
