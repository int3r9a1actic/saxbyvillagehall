Local updates, All Saints Church services, and digital newsletter archive.

All Saints Church, Saxby

All Saints Church is a Grade II* listed parish church situated immediately neighbouring our village hall. Together, the church and hall form the focal point of community life in Saxby All Saints.

Upcoming Church Services & Fellowship

  • 1st SUN
    09:15
    Holy Communion (Common Worship) Followed by fellowship coffee and tea in the village hall.
  • 3rd SUN
    10:30
    Family Morning Prayer & Hymns An informal, family-friendly service with children's activity corner.
  • SPECIAL
    18:00
    All Saints Patronal Festival & Songs of Praise Annual celebration with guest choir, followed by village refreshments.
Planning a Christening, Wedding, or Memorial?

Couples and families holding services at All Saints Church can book the village hall for receptions, teas, and celebrations just a few steps across the church path. Contact our coordinator to arrange a joint viewing.

Village Defibrillator

Located in the illuminated yellow cabinet on the external porch wall of the village hall. In an emergency dial 999 for the access code.

Community Book Swap

Our hall lobby features a thriving lending library. Take a paperback, leave a novel, or return when finished!

Village Garden Volunteers

Join our casual gardening work parties on the first Saturday morning of each month to keep the grounds and planters looking beautiful.
