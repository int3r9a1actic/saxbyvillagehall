Management Committee & Trustees

The dedicated volunteers who oversee governance, building care, finances, and community events.

Governance Structure

Governed by the Community, for the Community

Saxby All Saints Village Hall is a registered charity operated under a trust scheme. The hall receives no ongoing statutory central funding; our upkeep relies entirely upon hire revenues, local fundraising events, and grant awards.

The Management Committee comprises elected officers representing local residents alongside appointed representatives from key village bodies, including the Parish Council and Parochial Church Council (PCC).

Volunteer Operations

Annual General Meeting (AGM)

The Annual General Meeting of Saxby All Saints Village Hall is held each year in May. All residents of the parish are warmly invited to attend, hear the annual financial report, ask questions, and elect committee officers.

Minutes of past AGMs and trustee meetings are available for inspection upon request by any local parishioner.
