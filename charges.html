Security Deposit & Bond

For evening parties, private functions, and weddings, a refundable security deposit of £50.00 (or £100.00 for parties involving alcohol) is payable at confirmation.

The deposit is refunded in full within 7 working days following your event, subject to the hall and kitchen being left clean, tidy, free of damage, and all waste removed in accordance with our Hirer Guidelines.

Payment Methods

We accept payments via Bank Transfer (BACS) or cheque payable to Saxby All Saints Village Hall. BACS invoice details will be provided on confirmation of your reservation.

Full balance is due 14 days prior to your booking date. For recurring clubs, monthly invoices are issued at the conclusion of each calendar month.
